At Intel's modem team (later acquired by Apple), I was the **sole developer** of the software that automated testing of the **XMM 7360 4G LTE modem** &mdash; the modem that shipped in the **iPhone 7 and 7 Plus**.

**What I built.** Multithreaded Python software that replaced slow manual test procedures with automated runs, integrated with **HP Application Lifecycle Management (HP ALM) / Quality Center**. It was used by teams across the **US, China and Germany**.

**Tech:** Python, multithreading, HP ALM / Quality Center.
