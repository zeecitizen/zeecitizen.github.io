**Context.** Intel's **XMM 7360 4G LTE modem** shipped in the **iPhone 7 and 7 Plus**. Validating it meant running large, repetitive test procedures that were slow to do by hand.

**What I did.** As **sole developer**, I built &mdash; over the course of a year &mdash; multithreaded Python software that automated those test procedures end to end, and integrated it with **HP Application Lifecycle Management (HP ALM) / Quality Center**. It was used by teams across the **US, China and Germany**.

**Tech:** Python, multithreading, HP ALM / Quality Center.

*Werkstudent role at Intel (modem team, later acquired by Apple) &mdash; sanitized overview.*
