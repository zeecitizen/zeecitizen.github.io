Allianz's Global Data &amp; Analytics (GD&amp;A) department drives the company's data-science and big-data work. To help the team keep pace with evolving big-data technology, this project benchmarked state-of-the-art machine-learning algorithms in **local versus distributed** environments, measuring how model performance and training behaviour change as the workload is scaled out.
