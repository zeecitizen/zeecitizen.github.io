Recovering data structures from C/C++ binaries is central to forensic analysis and reverse engineering, yet it is very hard once the detailed pointer-structure information is lost at compile time. This review paper examines the research on "MemPick", a tool that detects and classifies dynamic data structures (lists, trees, graphs) from the heap at runtime.
